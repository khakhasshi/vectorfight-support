Flight support / 飛行支援

For a bug report or gameplay question, open a support issue. Include your device, OS version, game version and steps to reproduce. Please do not post passwords, personal details or sensitive screenshots; issues are public.

遇到問題或需要遊玩協助，請透過上方連結提出問題，附上裝置、系統版本、遊戲版本與重現步驟。問題內容為公開，請勿張貼密碼或私人資料。

Getting started / 開始飛行

Choose Flight School from the main menu to practice. Settings provides control and difficulty options. In the hangar, choose an aircraft and configure its loadout before launching.

在主選單選擇「飛行學校」練習操控。可在設定中調整操控與難度，出擊前於機庫選擇戰機及掛載。

Local duels / 區域網路對決

Use the same Wi-Fi or local network on both devices. Allow Local Network access when asked. One player hosts a room; the other joins. If no room appears, check network permission and whether the router isolates connected devices. You can also choose an AI opponent.

兩部裝置需連接同一區域網路，並允許區域網路存取。一位玩家建立房間，另一位加入。若找不到房間，請檢查權限及路由器是否隔離裝置；也可改選 AI 敵手。

Audio and assistance / 聲音與輔助

Settings lets you adjust sound and voice alerts. Select English or Traditional Chinese in the language options. Training and guidance options can be adjusted to suit your experience.

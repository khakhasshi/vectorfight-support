Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Effective October 6, 2026 · Applies to VectorFight for iOS and macOS

Gameplay data stays with you

VectorFight does not operate a developer account service, advertising system, tracking service or developer analytics backend. The app does not collect personal data for the developer. Settings, campaign progress, records and loadouts are stored on your device. Where iCloud is available, supported save progress may synchronize through your Apple iCloud account using Apple's services; the developer does not receive or access that iCloud data.

Local-network multiplayer

When you host or join a local duel, the app uses local-network discovery and exchanges room information and gameplay state directly with the other device. This is used to run the match, not sent to a developer server. You can decline Local Network permission and use single-player modes.

Photos and support

Images you choose to save are stored using the device's save or photo mechanisms. They are not uploaded to the developer. If you voluntarily contact support through GitHub, the issue and information you provide are handled by GitHub and the developer to respond to your request. Public issues are visible to others. GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that service.

Your choices

You can manage local-network and photo permissions in system settings, manage iCloud through Apple settings, and remove local app data by deleting the app and its stored data. For privacy questions, use the support page. Do not post sensitive information in public issues.
